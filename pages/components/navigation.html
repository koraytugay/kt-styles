Navigation

A row of page tabs under the page header, for switching between the pages of a small multi-page site. Mark the current page with aria-current="page".

Page tabs

Overview Reports History Settings
Overview Reports History

Multiple rows

For sites with many pages, group the links into .kt-nav__rows. On phones each row becomes its own horizontally scrolling line.

Overview Calendar History Statistics
Wishlist Archive Settings

Sticky

--sticky keeps the tabs at the top of the window while scrolling, with the page background behind them. It turns itself off on phones.

Typical page top

Page header first, then the tabs, then the content.
