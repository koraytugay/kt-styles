Components

Calendar

A month grid with a row of weekday names and one cell per day. Each day shows its date and whatever you put below it.

Month

Your script renders the cells. Days from the neighbouring months get .is-outside; today gets .is-today. The title and prev / next buttons are a .kt-split above the grid.

Class reference

ClassDescription
.kt-calendar7-column grid. Put 7 weekday cells first, then the day cells.
.kt-calendar__weekdayWeekday name (small uppercase).
.kt-calendar__dayA day cell; its content wraps below the date.
.kt-calendar__dateThe date number, on its own line at the top of the cell.
.is-outsideOn a day: from the previous or next month (dimmed).
.is-todayOn a day: today (accent edge and tint).
--kt-calendar-day-hMinimum cell height (104px; 56px on phones).
--kt-calendar-gapGap between cells (6px; 3px on phones).
