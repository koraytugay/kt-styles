Components

Forms

Inputs, selects, checkboxes, labelled fields, the filter toolbar and the segmented control. Every control is 32px tall, the same as buttons.

Inputs & selects

Textarea, sizes & states

Fields

A .kt-field pairs a label with a control, with an optional hint underneath. --inline puts the label on the left, which is the toolbar style.

Checkboxes

.kt-check wraps an input and its text in one clickable label. --boxed makes it look like a button.

Segmented control

Pick one of a few options: time ranges, view modes, units. Mark the selected button with .is-active and aria-pressed="true". The library is CSS only, so your script has to move those when the selection changes.

Multi-select with series colors

Several buttons can be active at once, for example to toggle chart series on and off. Set --kt-segmented-active-bg on a button to give its active state the color of its series.

Toolbar

The filter bar above a list or grid. Each .kt-toolbar__row is a wrapping line of controls. On phones the rows turn into a 2-column grid; .kt-field--grow and .kt-toolbar__full items take a full line.
