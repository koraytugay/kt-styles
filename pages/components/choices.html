Components

Choices

The options of a single-answer question: a round key (A, B, C …) and the answer. One click answers, so there is no selection mark; after answering, the key shows the result. For picking items to keep or compare, use the selectable card instead.

List

One option per line. Use it when the answers are sentences or longer phrases.

Row

.kt-choices--row puts short answers (numbers, single words) side by side in equal columns. They wrap when there is no room; set --kt-choices-min for a different minimum width.

Stacked

.kt-choice--stacked puts the key above a centred answer. Use it in a row when the answers are pictures.

States

aria-pressed="true" marks a pick that has no result yet. After answering, the right option gets .kt-choice--success (key ✓), a wrong pick .kt-choice--danger (key ✕), and every option is disabled: the others fade.
