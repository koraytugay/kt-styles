Foundations

Colors

A neutral, GitHub-like palette with a single blue accent. Every value is a CSS variable and is redefined for dark mode. Toggle the theme (top right) to see the values change.

Surfaces

White surfaces on a light grey page. Edges use --kt-border, lines inside a component the lighter --kt-divider, and controls the darker --kt-border-strong. Sunken is for image wells and inset areas.

--kt-bg
--kt-surface
--kt-surface-hover
--kt-surface-sunken
--kt-border
--kt-border-strong
--kt-divider

Text

Three levels of emphasis. Use secondary for supporting copy and muted for captions and metadata.

--kt-text
--kt-text-secondary
--kt-text-muted
--kt-text-inverse

Brand & interactive

Primary is near-black (inverted in dark mode): active nav tabs, primary buttons. Accent is blue: links, focus rings, highlights.

--kt-primary
--kt-accent
--kt-accent-hover
--kt-accent-soft

Semantic tones

Each tone comes in three parts: solid for fills and icons, -soft for tinted backgrounds, -text for readable text on the soft background.

--kt-success
--kt-warning
--kt-danger
--kt-info

Categorical

Bright, fixed colors for badges and tags. For charts and data, prefer the calmer series palette above. They stay the same in both themes.

Radii, borders & shadows

Surfaces are defined by a 1px --kt-border, never by shadows. Shadows exist only for layers that float above the page: popovers (--kt-shadow-lg), modals (--kt-shadow-xl) and badges over images.

--kt-radius-sm
--kt-radius-md
--kt-radius-lg
--kt-radius-full
--kt-shadow-lg
--kt-shadow-xl
