Foundations

Colors

A neutral, GitHub-like palette with a single blue accent. Every value is a CSS variable and is redefined for dark mode. Toggle the theme (top right) to see the values change.

Color language

Every color answers one question. Pick the role first, then the token.

RoleTokensUse for
Neutral (default)--kt-text*, --kt-border*, --kt-surface*Values, labels, headings, plain numbers. A number is text-colored unless it is a signed outcome.
Interactive--kt-primary, --kt-accent*Links, focus, active controls. Never data.
Outcome--kt-success*, --kt-danger*, --kt-warning*Gain/loss, up/down, good/bad, status. Never a category or a series name.
Data series--kt-series-1..7Categories and series, in order. A chart's single subject is always --kt-series-1.
Reference--kt-chart-reference, dashedBenchmarks, averages, targets: something the data is compared against.
Chart furniture--kt-chart-grid, -axis, -label, -cursorGridlines, zero line, tick labels, hover crosshair.
Diverging scale--kt-scale-pos-1..3, --kt-scale-neg-1..3Heatmap cells and other signed magnitudes.
Categorical--kt-orange, --kt-blue…Badges and tags only, when a label needs its own identity.

Surfaces

White surfaces on a light grey page. Edges use --kt-border, lines inside a component the lighter --kt-divider, and controls the darker --kt-border-strong. Sunken is for image wells and inset areas.

Diverging scale

Three steps on each side of zero, for heatmap cells and other signed magnitudes. Steps 1 and 2 take --kt-success-text / --kt-danger-text, step 3 takes --kt-text-on-color. Zero or no data uses --kt-surface-sunken with --kt-text-muted.

Radii, borders & shadows

Surfaces are defined by a 1px --kt-border, never by shadows. Shadows exist only for layers that float above the page: popovers (--kt-shadow-lg), modals (--kt-shadow-xl) and badges over images.
